# Community code of conduct

The Advatek Labs community should be a practical, welcoming place for
installers, hobbyists, engineers, and first-time contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical facts directly.
- Welcome questions at different experience levels.
- Give actionable review feedback and explain safety concerns clearly.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses and confidential material.
- Keep hardware testing honest; distinguish observed evidence from assumption.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing,
credential sharing, unsafe pressure on testers, or publishing confidential
protocol material is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Serious
conduct concerns should be raised privately with the repository maintainers.
Public issues are reserved for project work.
